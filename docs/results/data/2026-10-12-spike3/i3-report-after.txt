Lattice improve: proposals from the event log (no model; the owner decides)

Method: Frequency x cost, the I-R method (docs/results/2026-10-12-i-r-improvement-ranking.md): events are clustered by signal_key or cause; f = occurrences; c = the mean cost of one occurrence in wall seconds, or in list-price dollars only when the cluster recorded no seconds (never converted); score = f x c. Seconds-scored clusters (class A) rank above dollar-scored ones (B), above clusters with no recorded cost (C, ordered by f, not a score). Items closed in improvements.jsonl are left out. At most N proposals; one that touches the checker or the spec language is needs_owner and never drafted. The owner decides; nothing is merged automatically.

Blind spots:
  - human time: approvals, diagnosis and hand merges are not a cost here (human events are counted, not timed)
  - risk and correctness: a wrong answer or a hidden bug costs no seconds, so it ranks by frequency only
  - recurrence: f counts past occurrences, not future ones; a one-off scores like a habit
  - cost to fix is not an input (a cheap fix and an expensive one with the same signal rank the same)
  - causes an old record cannot state (other) and signals no event records (a workaround that hides an error)
  - one project's log: n is small, so a ranking can overfit that project
